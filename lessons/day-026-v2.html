01 — Grammar and foundations · 20 min

5 min: present simple for routines; past simple for finished past events; present continuous for actions happening now; present perfect for past actions relevant now; will for future decisions. After did or will, use the base verb. Use a/an for a nonspecific singular countable noun and the for a specific one.

10 min: say each answer before revealing it.

02 — Natural chunks · 15 min

5 min: read the cards and say each example. The first three are original Day 26 introductions; the others are reviews.

Let's narrow it down to...

NEW

Meaning: Reduce the number of possible causes or choices.

When to use: When several explanations are possible.

Everyday: Let's narrow it down to two shops.

Workplace: Let's narrow it down to the queue and database.

We can trace ... back to...

NEW

Meaning: Follow evidence to an earlier event or source.

When to use: When evidence connects something to its origin.

Everyday: We can trace the leak back to the kitchen.

Workplace: We can trace the delay back to a slow query.

Before we roll this out,...

NEW

Meaning: Introduce a step that must happen before a wider release.

When to use: When preparing to introduce a change.

Everyday: Before we roll this out, let's explain the new rules.

Workplace: Before we roll this out, let's test it.

I'd start by...

REVIEW · DAY 25

Meaning: Describe the first action you would take.

When to use: When suggesting a plan.

Everyday: I'd start by cleaning the kitchen.

Workplace: I'd start by checking the logs.

Let's flag this before...

REVIEW · DAY 23

Meaning: Draw attention to a concern before an event.

When to use: When a problem needs attention early.

Everyday: Let's flag this before the trip.

Workplace: Let's flag this before the release.

This is grounded in...

REVIEW · DAY 19

Meaning: This is supported by facts or evidence.

When to use: When explaining the basis of an idea.

Everyday: This is grounded in our experience.

Workplace: This is grounded in the trace data.

Context clue: The investigation is gaining traction. Is it progressing?

Yes. Gaining traction means starting to make progress.

03 — Intensive substitutions · 30 min

Lexical substitution · 10 min

Base: I check the logs every morning.

Replace logs with metrics — say the entire sentence first

I check the metrics every morning.

Replace every morning with after lunch — say the entire sentence first

I check the metrics after lunch.

Replace metrics with error reports — say the entire sentence first

I check the error reports after lunch.

Replace after lunch with before the meeting — say the entire sentence first

I check the error reports before the meeting.

Replace error reports with deployment records — say the entire sentence first

I check the deployment records before the meeting.

Repeat the whole sequence without looking. Then make two original variations and say them aloud.

Grammatical substitution · 10 min

Base: I check the logs every day.

Change to yesterday: past simple — say the entire sentence first

I checked the logs yesterday.

Make it negative — say the entire sentence first

I didn't check the logs yesterday.

Ask a question with you — say the entire sentence first

Did you check the logs yesterday?

Answer about she, right now — say the entire sentence first

She is checking the logs right now.

Change to already: present perfect — say the entire sentence first

She has already checked the logs.

Change to tomorrow: future — say the entire sentence first

She will check the logs tomorrow.

Repeat the whole sequence without looking. Then make two original variations and say them aloud.

Mixed substitution · 10 min

Base: We need to check the queue.

Change queue to database — say the entire sentence first

We need to check the database.

Change to yesterday — say the entire sentence first

We needed to check the database yesterday.

Change we to he and make it negative — say the entire sentence first

He didn't need to check the database yesterday.

Ask a question with you — say the entire sentence first

Did you need to check the database yesterday?

Answer with I, now, and metrics — say the entire sentence first

I am checking the metrics now.

Change to already and traces — say the entire sentence first

I have already checked the traces.

Repeat the whole sequence without looking. Then make two original variations and say them aloud.

05 — Writing and correction · 10 min

Write four sentences: what happened yesterday, what you are checking now, what you have already checked, and what you will do next. Then turn one sentence into a question and another into a negative. Check auxiliaries and verb forms.

Reveal model after writing

The API became slower yesterday. I am checking the traces now. I have already checked the error rate. I will check the database tomorrow. Did the API become slower yesterday? I have not checked the database yet.
