## Daily contract (90 minutes)
- Foundations 20: explain one primary concept in plain English, meaning, purpose, formula, affirmative/negative/question forms, pronoun variation, common errors with corrections, contrasts, everyday and professional examples, hidden answers. Explain why auxiliaries change main verb forms. Retain practical numbers, spelling, dates, money and prepositions within this time.
- Chunks 15: exactly three verified NEW and three REVIEW; explain grammatical patterns and adapt subjects and tenses. Retain noticing and ambiguity (5), story (5), recall (3), recovery (2).
- Substitution 30: lexical, grammatical, mixed, ten minutes each; 4–6 cumulative correct cues each, hidden answers, linked agreement changes together with explanation, independent variations.
- Speaking 15: small talk 5, scenario 8, correction/retry 2. Both AI Voice and Solo Recorder. One question at a time, actual corrections only, increasingly unpredictable follow-ups.
- Writing 10: independent original construction, transformations and connected explanations; final task independent, answers hidden. Reduce hints over time.

## Progression and reviews
Use the daily roadmap topic, dependency and review references. Review offsets 1, 3, 7, 14, 30 days within existing blocks; prioritize due topics and contrast confusable forms. Every seventh day cumulative checkpoint; Day 150 final independent production. Each week increases independent sentence opportunities across speaking/writing/drills (not additional homework); hints fade and contextual unpredictability increases. Levels 1–6: recognition, guided construction, transformation, independent construction, spontaneous production, mixed production. Earlier levels remain available for review. Advanced forms get less time than high-frequency forms. No artificial speed pressure or vocabulary inflation. Distinguish introduced, recognized, assisted practice, independent practice and spontaneous demonstration; only actual evidence supports learner status. Never invent metrics.

Preserve scenario rotation, metaphor focus days (combine grammar topic with figurative theme), English-only content, privacy and historical chunk verification. Use incoming JSON pipeline described in DAILY_RUNBOOK.md.
