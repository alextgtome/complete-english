START HERE · FREE 12-MINUTE PRACTICE

You understand English.
But speaking takes longer.

Maybe you can read books or follow technical conversations, but a useful phrase disappears halfway through a sentence. That doesn't necessarily mean you're translating in your head. You may need more practice retrieving and connecting natural language in real time.

This is a learning experiment, not a test or a fluency guarantee. No account or downloads required. You can practise on your own with a phone recorder.

Try the exercise
2 min — Notice

Recognise situations and patterns.

6 min — Substitute

Say full sentences with guided changes.

4 min — Recover

Continue even when a word disappears.

1. Notice three natural chunks 2 minutes

Connect each expression to a situation; read its example out loud.

I'm thinking about + -ing

Estou pensando em... · Quando usar: ao considerar uma possibilidade.

I'm thinking about taking the train.

It turns out that...

Acontece que... · Quando usar: ao relatar uma descoberta inesperada.

It turns out that the shop closes early.

What I mean is...

O que quero dizer é... · Quando usar: ao explicar melhor uma ideia.

What I mean is, I'd prefer a simpler option.

2. Guided substitution 6 minutes

Cumulative drill: each change applies to the previous sentence. Read the starting sentence, see one cue, hide it, say the entire sentence, then reveal the answer. No invention required.

After each sequence, repeat all the full sentences from memory at a comfortable pace. The objective is natural retrieval, not speed.

3. Keep speaking 4 minutes

Scenario: You planned to meet someone on Saturday, but something changed. Explain what happened out loud for 45–60 seconds.

Now repeat. Deliberately imagine you cannot recall one useful word. Continue with a simpler expression or a short explanation. Do not restart the entire sentence.

Reveal recovery phrases after attempting

What I mean is, we needed to choose a different time.

Let me put it another way: Saturday wasn't possible anymore.

Self-check: Was your main point clear? Did you keep speaking rather than restarting?
